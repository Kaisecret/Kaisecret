Features & Experiences

Designed For Your Comfort

Swimming Pool

Dive into our crystal-clear lap and kiddie pools, perfect for daytime fun or late-afternoon lounging under the tropical sun.

Beach Access

Enjoy direct, private access to the pristine Hamtic shoreline. Ideal for peaceful morning walks and vibrant sunset viewing.

Family Cottages

Spacious, beautifully designed cabanas and cottages tailored for families and groups wanting to stay together comfortably.

Air-Conditioned Rooms

Retreat from the tropical heat into our fully air-conditioned rooms, featuring plush bedding and modern conveniences for a restful sleep.

Event Space

Versatile indoor and outdoor venues completely equipped to host your weddings, reunions, birthdays, and corporate retreats.

Parking Area

Safe, secure, and ample on-site parking available exclusively for our resort guests and event attendees.

Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected with complimentary high-speed internet access available in all rooms and major public areas.

Savor freshly prepared local delicacies and refreshing beverages at our on-site dining areas with stunning ocean views.

Scenic Photo Spots

Unwind in our lush gardens, shaded hammocks, and quiet open-air lounges. Designed specifically to help you disconnect from the rush and reconnect with nature's tranquility.
