Welcome to Paradise

Discover Your Tropical Escape in Antique

Nestled along the pristine shores of Banawon, Hamtic, ORR Beach Resort offers a serene getaway for families, friends, and couples. Experience the perfect blend of modern comfort and natural beauty as you relax by the sea.

Whether you're looking for a peaceful retreat, an exciting beach adventure, or a picturesque venue for your special events, our resort provides an unforgettable experience with stunning sunset views and warm hospitality.
